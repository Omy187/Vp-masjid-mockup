Religious Guidance & Q&A

How do I consult an Imam for religious fatwas or personal counseling?

You can meet our Imams in person following Daily Zuhr or Isha prayers, or send a written query through our contact form.

Are ladies' prayer facilities open for all five daily prayers?

Yes, dedicated sister facilities are accessible during all daily congregational times and Jumu'ah services.
